Please save your Resume as a PDF

11 Feb 2025

Part of my job is to hire and so I end up looking at a lot of resumes.

The vast majority of resumes are PDF files, although I sometimes encounter something different, like .docx.

I believe a PDF file is the best format because it has several advantages -

  1. Interoperability - The PDF enjoys support from a wide range of applications and software. Almost every piece of software knows how to open it or export to it (where applicable). Compare that to a .docx which my browser can't even natively open (causing me the added inconvenience of downloading the file and opening it in another application).
  2. Formatting - PDF files allow for richer formatting including text, images, and basic styling. It is a compiled format that is optimized for presentation. Misspelled words don't appear with red squiggly lines everywhere. A PDF also ensures that your document renders consistently on every platform so that it looks the same however it is viewed.
  3. (Lack of) Editability - PDF files can not be (easily) edited by accident (or maliciously).

I encourage all candidates to share their resume in PDF format.
